## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Mostly template, with one authored thread. The incumbent kit is the standard 2023–25 dev-portfolio look:
- warm paper with ink, and a coral dot after the wordmark
- Manrope 800 headlines with one Georgia-italic word each ("iterations.", "curiosity.", "soil.", "talk.")
- mono "02 / …" eyebrows and a live local clock
- a typed greeting, a magnetic CTA and a toolkit marquee
- numbered project rows with a cursor-following preview
- a closing "Got an idea? Let's talk."

You could swap in another student's name and it would still work. The copy is generic as well. "Curious" or "curiosity" appears four or more times (brand label, hero, About h1, stage caption).

What only Joel has: the SenSOIL "It started in the soil." section, with real photos, a real place (Brgy. Taptap) and real awards; the 3D hobby objects; and the bespoke project illustrations. All of it is buried on About. The dark-suit studio headshot is the largest visual mass on Home, and it pulls against the "curious creative" voice.

**Deterministic scan:**
- Source scan (`src`, `index.html`): 0 findings.
- Rendered URL scan: 13 findings at 1280px and 15 at 390px.

## Overall Impression

This is well-engineered, accessible and coherent. It's also safe, and it hides its best material. The craft is above typical student work: reduced-motion handling, focus management, WebGL fallback, an sr-only marquee. The problem is what the pages lead with: a generic hero, a stock-feeling headshot, a marquee, and project cards that are illustrations instead of proof. The biggest opportunity is to make the real work and the SenSOIL story the spine of Home.

## What's Working

1. **Motion with real accessibility discipline.**
   - `MotionConfig reducedMotion="user"`.
   - TypingText has an sr-only copy and holds its width.
   - The marquee has a pause button, a static fallback and an sr-only list.
   - Focus moves to the h1 on route change.
   - The WebGL stage falls back to an illustration through an error boundary.
2. **A cohesive, owned illustration system.** Coral, blue, lime and lavender with thick ink outlines, matching the UI accents. The four covers read as a family.
3. **The SenSOIL section is authored storytelling.** It has a specific headline, a concrete place and outcome, a captioned carousel with swipe, keys and dots, and a numbered awards list.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The work section shows illustrations, not proof.**
- **What:** Three of four covers are labeled "concept art", and the dialog repeats the disclaimer. The dialog's primary dark button is the `mailto:` "Let's talk about this". "Live demo" and "View on GitHub" are small text links (`src/components/ProjectDialog.tsx:118-134`). Cards don't say what Joel personally built.
- **Why it matters:** On a portfolio the work must lead. A recruiter can't judge skill from illustrations. Clario's live demo, the strongest proof on the site, is a tertiary link.
- **Fix:**
  - Add real screenshots or short recordings (emulator captures of Locker and Aspire, the live Clario UI), and keep the art as a secondary accent.
  - Add a "My role / what I built" line to each card.
  - Make Live demo (or GitHub) the primary dialog action and contact secondary.
  - Surface "Live demo" on the Clario card.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify, then /impeccable layout

**[P1] The composition is interchangeable, and Joel's specifics are buried.**
- **What:** Home is hero + headshot + 5-item marquee + work list, which is the template. SenSOIL, Cebu and the 3D hobbies sit on About.
- **Why it matters:** Nothing on the first screen is memorable or specific to Joel, so visitors leave with "another dev portfolio".
- **Fix:**
  - Rewrite the hero from a real fact, such as the thread from the soil sensor to building apps.
  - Cut the marquee, which duplicates the /work skills.
  - Bring one authored element onto Home: the SenSOIL thread or the 3D stage.
  - Retire "curious" from three of its four uses.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable bolder, then /impeccable distill

**[P2] Layout breaks at the grid and on mobile.**
- **What:**
  - (a) `.project-grid` is three columns with four projects (`src/styles.css:715-719`). SteelBound sits alone, with two-thirds of its row empty at 1440px and at 900px.
  - (b) At 390px the 4:5 headshot fills about one screen, and "Things I'm building" starts about 1.6 viewports down.
  - (c) Home work-row thumbnails exist only in the mouse-only hover preview (`src/components/WorkList.tsx:53,63`), so touch users get a text-only list.
- **Why it matters:** The orphaned card reads as unfinished. Mobile visitors scroll past a photo before they see any work.
- **Fix:**
  - Use a 2×2 grid, or one large featured project plus three.
  - At ≤760px, crop the portrait to about 1:1 at 40–50% width beside the name, or move it below the work.
  - Add inline thumbnails on touch.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout, then /impeccable adapt

**[P2] Type is undersized and tap targets are small.**
- **What:**
  - Body copy measures 13–15px, buttons and links 12px, and mono labels 11px. The detector flagged `tiny-text` on `.project-credit` and `.about-visual-note`.
  - Tap targets at 390px are small: socials and text links 17px tall, carousel dots 20px, "A little about me" 26px.
  - There is no `text-wrap: balance/pretty`, which causes the straggler warnings.
- **Why it matters:** Strain for readers, misses for thumbs, and orphaned words in headings.
- **Fix:**
  - Body ≥16px, buttons and links 14–15px, mono labels ≥12px.
  - Pad link hit areas to 44px.
  - Add `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `pretty` on paragraphs.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable typeset, then /impeccable adapt

**[P2] Dead-end page endings and an inconsistent "Let's talk".**
- **What:** Home and Work end on a copyright and trademark footer, with no email, résumé or closing CTA. The dialog CTA uses `mailto:` and fails silently without a mail client.
- **Why it matters:** The peak-end rule fails on two of three pages, and a recruiter finishing the Work page has nowhere to go.
- **Fix:**
  - Add a compact closing band on Home and Work: the email as text, a résumé PDF, GitHub and LinkedIn.
  - Route the dialog CTA to the QuickContact panel, prefilled with "Re: {project}".
  - Replace the trademark note with something useful.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout, then /impeccable harden
